DeckVault GitHub

Self-hosted Game Library Manager for handheld consoles - track installs across SD cards, HDDs, and SSDs

If you own a handheld with more than one SD card, you've asked yourself this. You want to play something, but you can't remember if it's on the card that's currently in the slot, the one on your desk, or the device storage.

DeckVault is a self-hosted app where you add your SD cards, external SSDs, and internal storage, then list which games are on each one.

Game info and cover art are fetched automatically from IGDB, so your library looks good without any manual work.

And when you want to play something, you'll know exactly which card or drive it's on.
